# Sample brief: food ordering and delivery (fictional)

A regional food-delivery start-up connects customers, restaurants and couriers.

**Today.** Orders arrive at a restaurant tablet over a polling connection; when the tablet is offline the order is lost. Couriers are assigned by a dispatcher over chat. At lunch and dinner peaks the single application slows down for everyone.

**Target.**
- Customers browse restaurants and menus, place and pay for orders, track the delivery and rate the order.
- Restaurant staff manage their menu, accept orders and mark them ready.
- Couriers accept delivery jobs and confirm delivery.

**Quality targets.** No lost orders; order placement under 2 seconds for 95% of requests; peaks of 15 times the average load.

**External systems.** Card payment provider, maps and routing provider.
