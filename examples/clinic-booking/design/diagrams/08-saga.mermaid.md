```mermaid
  n_appointment_service--)n_scheduling_service: 2. Hold the slot for 10 minutes

  n_insurance_service->>n_insurer: 5. Cover check
  n_insurance_service--)n_appointment_service: 6. Cover confirmed
  n_appointment_service--)n_payment_service: 7. Collect the deposit
  n_payment_service->>n_payment_provider: 8. Charge the deposit with the same key
  n_payment_service--)n_appointment_service: 9. Deposit captured
  n_appointment_service--)n_scheduling_service: 10. Commit the slot
  n_appointment_service--)n_notification_service: 11. Appointment confirmed
```

- Numbers give the order. Red messages are compensations.
